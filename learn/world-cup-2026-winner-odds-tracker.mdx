## How to Read These Numbers

A probability of 18% on a tournament winner means:
- The market collectively believes that team has roughly an 18% chance of winning.
- A $1 contract on this team can be bought for ~$0.18 and pays $1 if they win — a 4.5x return.
- If the price moves to $0.30 after they win their group, you can sell at $0.30 — a 67% gain without waiting for the final.

**Critical:** the market is not a forecast. It's a price aggregating opinions from people willing to put money on those opinions. Sometimes it's right. Sometimes it's slow to update. It's the best single proxy available, not a guarantee.

## Pre-Tournament vs Tournament Pricing

Pre-tournament prices reflect:
- Form leading up to the tournament
- Squad announcements and injuries
- Historical conversion rates of pre-tournament favorites
- Draw position (which side of the bracket you're on)

Tournament prices reflect all of the above **plus**:
- Group stage results (already done by Round of 16)
- Bracket clarity (who you'd have to beat)
- In-tournament injuries and red cards

Tournament prices move much faster. Expect 10–30% daily moves on contracts during knockout rounds.

## What to Watch

The numbers in the table above will shift. To read them well during the tournament:

- **Top-5 stability.** If the same 5 teams remain in the top 5 across two updates, the market is converging. Rapid reshuffles signal real news flowing in.
- **Bracket-path bias.** A team's winner-price doesn't only reflect their quality — it also reflects how favorable their potential bracket path is. After group draws settle, paths re-price.
- **Sum-of-probabilities.** All winner shares across all 48 teams should sum near 1.00. A persistent gap above 1.00 indicates fee/spread overhead; below 1.00 indicates thin trade.
- **Cross-market consistency.** A team trading 0.15 in tournament-winner should also be a top-tier favorite in "make the final" and "make the semis." Inconsistencies are sometimes a real edge — or sometimes thin liquidity in one market.
